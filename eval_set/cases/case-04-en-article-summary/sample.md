# Nordwind Analytics Ships FlowBoard 4.0 With Real-Time Collaboration

*Published: September 15, 2026 — Fictional Industry Wire*

Nordwind Analytics, a fictional data-tools startup based in Portland, announced FlowBoard 4.0 on Monday, the latest version of its dashboard platform. The release adds real-time multiplayer editing, a feature the company says was its most requested since 2024.

## What's new

- Real-time co-editing for up to 50 concurrent users per board
- A redesigned query builder with natural-language preview
- Version history with one-click restore (retained for 90 days)
- EU data residency option for enterprise customers

## Pricing changes

FlowBoard's Team plan rises from $18 to $22 per seat, while the Pro plan stays at $45. Nordwind says existing customers keep current pricing until their next renewal.

## Context

Analysts note the launch puts pressure on rivals Meltwater Labs and Quillo, both of which shipped collaboration features earlier this year but at higher price points. Nordwind claims 12,000 paying teams as of Q2 2026, up 34% year over year — figures shared in a fictional press briefing.

"Teams told us dashboards are where decisions stall," said fictional CEO Dana Reyes. "4.0 is about making the dashboard the place where the decision happens."
